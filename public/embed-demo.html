Embed demo

Each of these is an iframe of /embed?id=<YACPDB id> and nothing else — the board, and what it takes to solve it. The markup under each one is the markup that draws it.

Inside the frame the composer and the source stay out of sight until the solve is decided, as a solving tourney's diagram sheet does. A page that would rather have them in view writes them beside the diagram, as here.

Other sizes

The board takes the width it is given, or the height left over once the lines around it have taken theirs — whichever is smaller. None of these scrolls.
